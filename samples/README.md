# Samples

Both samples are **synthetic test messages** written for this project. Neither was sent by the company it names.

- `scam_impersonation.txt` follows common recruitment-scam patterns: brand impersonation, a gmail sender, a UPI "refundable deposit", and a 24-hour deadline.
- `genuine_offer.txt` follows the shape of a legitimate offer: sent from the company's own domain, no money requested, no pressure tactics, and acceptance through the official careers site. Parakh should not flag it as a scam. You can replace it with a real offer from your own inbox (redact your phone number, address and ID numbers first).
